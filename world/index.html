how it works

three steps, no wallet
1

say hatch

tweet "@trefxworld hatch" and a creature is seeded from your username. one per person. it lives at the village.

2

feed it, send it

"feed it tacos", "train spd", "send it to the bog". it walks there on this map, finds loot, meets other creatures, writes a diary about you.

raid together

every hour a boss shows up. every creature at home fights it live in the arena. cheer to buff yours. top damage gets the loot.
